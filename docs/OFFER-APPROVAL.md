# JIZO WORKS — Offer Approval Contract

Public monetization must remain fail-closed.

## States

- `candidate`: source or campaign discovered; never rendered publicly as an ad.
- `approved`: agreement/approval confirmed and a real tracking or sponsor URL is available.
- `paused`: previously approved but intentionally not rendered.
- `rejected`: unsuitable, expired, rights/quality issue, or commercially rejected.

## Approval requirements

Before `approved`:

1. Confirm the advertiser/program is real.
2. Confirm JIZO WORKS has been accepted where acceptance is required.
3. Use the actual issued tracking/sponsor URL, not an application URL.
4. Confirm disclosure requirements.
5. Confirm destination is HTTPS.
6. Confirm the offer is suitable for the page/audience.
7. Do not publish invented commission terms.
8. Re-check changing campaign terms at the source.

The public site only renders `approved` offers.
